Privacy policy

Scope & contact

This policy applies to ClassWay, independently developed by Richis. Privacy inquiries: richisjin@gmail.com.

Schedule & local data

ClassWay reads courses from a webpage or HTML file you select and locally stores course codes, titles, sections, enrollment, meeting times and locations, instructors, exam and drop dates, colors, reminder and sync preferences, and app-managed calendar identifiers. Original HTML is not retained as the schedule file. Widgets read a local App Group copy. This version does not upload schedules to developer servers or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. System backups may process local files according to your settings.

Accounts & website login

ClassWay does not read or save username or password fields. You sign in on the school's website. iOS WebKit manages sessions, cookies, caches, and website storage. Visiting the website communicates with school servers under their privacy rules. A school website session is not a ClassWay account.

Calendars, reminders & maps

When you choose to sync, ClassWay uses EventKit to identify and manage its course events. Events may include course, location, and instructor information. Your calendar account may sync to the cloud according to system settings. Class reminders are local notifications. Opening directions sends the location query to the map service under its terms and privacy policy.

Feedback email

Feedback is sent through your email service only after you confirm. The developer receives your sender address and the text or attachments you voluntarily provide to investigate and reply. They are retained until the issue is resolved or follow-up is no longer needed, except where legally required. ClassWay does not automatically attach schedules, complete HTML, cookies, or logs. Avoid sensitive information. Contact the support email to request deletion of submitted feedback.

Manage & delete

In Calendar & data, clear the local schedule, website data, or app-created course calendars. Clearing the schedule cancels app notifications but does not remove Apple Calendar events; remove course calendars separately. Website data remains until cleared or managed by the system. Revoke permissions in system Settings.

Policy updates

If data processing or external services change, this policy will be updated and users will be informed as required.
